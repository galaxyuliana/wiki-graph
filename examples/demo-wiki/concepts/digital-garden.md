A digital garden is a collection of notes tended over time — less a blog,
more a greenhouse. Ideas start as seedlings and are pruned into
[[evergreen-notes]].

Gardens borrow their bones from the [[zettelkasten]] tradition and get their
life from [[backlinks]].
